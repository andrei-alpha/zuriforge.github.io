Piece 01 Custom Home Wellness • Dual-Use Enclosure

Custom Built-In Shower Sauna

Custom-engineered directly inside an existing walk-in bathroom shower enclosure while keeping the everyday shower completely functional. The entire cabin was built from the ground up—starting with the structural timber framing and moisture-tight FF-PIR thermal insulation boards, followed by horizontal interior wood wall paneling, a full-width seating bench, and removable slatted floor duckboards. A purchased tempered glass door and fixed glass partition were fitted into the custom wooden portal alongside a wall-mounted electric stone heater and warm indirect LED strip lighting tucked beneath the bench. To withstand intense steam cycles and direct shower splash alike, every wooden surface was thoroughly conditioned with specialized sauna oils imported from Finland.

Piece 02 Workshop & Home Furniture • Beech

Folding Beech 3-Step Stool

A sturdy three-step folding ladder chair crafted from solid European beech wood (Fagus sylvatica), featuring two lower treads, a deep top standing platform, and a gently arched top rail. Stainless pivot bolts along the upper stiles allow the rear A-frame support legs to fold flat against the main frame for compact storage.

Folding three-step wooden stool handcrafted in solid beech with wide top platform and sculpted top rail. Click to enlarge
Piece 03 Bathroom Furnishings • Pine

Leaning Pine Bathroom Towel Ladder

A clean-lined six-rung leaning bathroom towel rack crafted from light pine wood to bring natural warmth against stone bathroom tiles. Each cylindrical wooden rung passes cleanly through precision-bored mortises in the slender side rails, leaving crisp proud dowel ends on both outsides.

Six-rung leaning bathroom towel ladder built from pale pine wood with through-dowel joinery. Click to enlarge
Piece 04 Resin Art & Serving • Walnut & Epoxy

Walnut & Ocean-Wave Epoxy Serving Board

A striking charcuterie and bread serving board pairing rich dark walnut timber on one flank with a multi-layered turquoise epoxy ocean pour on the other. Heat-gun-blown white cresting pigments create realistic cellular sea-foam lacing across deep teal depths while leaving pure solid walnut along the slicing zone.

Walnut and turquoise ocean-wave epoxy serving board featuring layered sea-foam resin lacing. Click to enlarge
Piece 05 Curved Lamination • Beech & Pine

Undulating Wave Cutting Board in Beech & Pine

A rhythmic cutting and serving board crafted from alternating strips of reddish-gold beech and pale yellow pine arranged in an undulating wave pattern. Repeating sinusoidal cuts and offset re-glue cycles cause the contrasting strips to weave smoothly across the length of the chamfered, oil-finished board.

Finished undulating wave cutting board crafted from contrasting beech and pine wood strips. Click to enlarge
Piece 06 Bespoke Gift • Tri-Species End-Grain

Walnut, Beech & Purpleheart End-Grain Cutting Board

A gift-wrapped end-grain butcher block uniting deep chocolate walnut blocks with crisp golden beech pinstripes and a bold royal purpleheart accent band. Completed with a continuous routed perimeter juice groove and conditioned with food-safe mineral oil before tying the red presentation ribbon.

End-grain cutting board in walnut, beech, and purpleheart with perimeter juice groove, wrapped as a gift. Click to enlarge
Piece 07 Culinary Butcher Block • End-Grain Mosaic

Checkerboard End-Grain Cutting Board

A thick, heavy-duty checkerboard end-grain butcher block built from hundreds of alternating light and dark hardwood cubes arranged in a crisp grid. Generously radiused corners and a deep perimeter juice groove make this vertical-grain workhorse equally suited for daily prep and roast carving.

Thick checkerboard end-grain cutting board featuring rounded corners and a routed perimeter juice groove. Click to enlarge
Piece 08 Table Presentation • Mediterranean Olive

Solid Olive Wood Pastry & Serving Board

A rectangular serving platter crafted from richly figured Mediterranean olive wood (Olea europaea), highlighting its dramatic caramel-and-umber grain ribbons. Finely sanded and oiled to a warm satin luster, the dense, naturally aromatic timber frames patisserie, cheeses, and tapas with effortless character.

Solid olive wood serving board displaying vivid Mediterranean grain swirls beneath gourmet pastries. Click to enlarge
Piece 09 Bathroom Small Craft • SaRaiFo Veneer

SaRaiFo Wood Veneer Slatted Soap Dish

A compact five-slat soap dish crafted from SaRaiFo (Save the RainForest) engineered wood veneer, prized for its sustainable exotic-style plum-and-charcoal grain lines. Softly rounded parallel bars elevated on two cross-feet promote rapid airflow and drainage so bar soap stays dry between uses.

Slatted bathroom soap dish crafted from purple-and-charcoal striped SaRaiFo reconstituted wood veneer. Click to enlarge
Piece 10 Sculptural Offcuts • 20 × 7 cm

Walnut & Beech Dachshund Figurine

A playful 20 cm × 7 cm decorative dachshund sculpture laminated from vertical offcuts of rich dark walnut and pale European beech. Strategic beech end-blocks highlight the upturned snout, tail base, and hind heel, finished with softened edges, a carved grin, and a dark inlaid eye.

Decorative 20 cm × 7 cm wooden dachshund figurine crafted from laminated walnut and beech blocks. Click to enlarge
Piece 11 Silhouette Carving • Solid Walnut

Hand-Cut Walnut Camel Figurine

A freestanding miniature dromedary camel figurine hand-cut from a single piece of solid walnut stock. Clean scroll-cut curves capture the poised neck, single hump, and delicate tail cleft while letting the warm diagonal walnut grain speak for itself.

Hand-cut solid walnut camel figurine photographed atop the live-edge olive wood river board. Click to enlarge
Piece 12 Laser Engraving • 5 mm Plywood

Alpine Ski Resort Coasters in 5 mm Plywood

A matched eight-piece set of round drink coasters cut from 5 mm plywood and laser-engraved with vintage alpine travel poster illustrations. Four classic European ski destinations—St. Moritz, Ischgl, Chamonix, and Zermatt—are rendered in crisp sepia relief with gondolas, peaks, and skiers.

Set of eight 5 mm plywood coasters laser-engraved with St. Moritz, Ischgl, Chamonix, and Zermatt ski motifs. Click to enlarge
Piece 13 Freehand Rotary Carving • Solid Wood

Hand-Carved Dremel Wood Coasters

A set of four thick round wooden coasters carved entirely freehand using a Dremel rotary burr before sealing on yellow painter's pyramids. Each disc captures a playful line-etched subject: a wide-eyed owl, a curious pug, a golfer at the top of a swing, and the iconic Matterhorn ridge.
